FAQ

Common questions

How quickly can LoomOps deploy an AI agent? +

Most agents are live within 5–14 business days. Simple single-task agents can be deployed faster. More complex multi-system workflows with custom integrations typically take 2–4 weeks for full deployment.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with LoomOps? +

Not at all. We handle all the technical build, integration, and maintenance. Your team only needs to know your business — we handle everything else. We have a simple onboarding process that captures your requirements in plain language.

How do your AI agents integrate with our existing tools? +

We connect natively with 100+ tools including HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Gmail, Notion, Google Workspace, Zapier, Make, and more. If you use something more custom, we use APIs to build a bespoke integration.

What happens if an agent makes a mistake? +

Every agent includes configurable human-in-the-loop checkpoints for sensitive actions. We also build monitoring dashboards so you can see everything your agents do in real-time. All agents include rollback capabilities.

Is our company data secure with LoomOps? +

Yes. We follow enterprise-grade security practices, including data encryption at rest and in transit, minimal data retention policies, and role-based access controls. We operate as a data processor under your privacy policies and can execute DPAs.

How is LoomOps different from using Zapier or Make directly? +

Zapier and Make are rule-based automation tools — they follow a fixed logic tree. LoomOps agents use LLMs to reason, adapt, and make decisions based on context. They can handle ambiguity, unexpected inputs, and nuanced situations that break traditional automation.
